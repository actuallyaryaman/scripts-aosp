# Attribution and licensing

This toolkit includes original helpers and patches derived from third-party ROM
projects. Existing copyright, SPDX and patch author notices remain in the files.
No single license is asserted for every file in this collection.

Camera patch origins, commits and checksums are recorded in
`../maintainer/camera-port/upstream.json` and the associated patch files.
Separate App Sound retains its Apache-2.0 SPDX notices. Emoji and Lindroid sources
and pinned references are documented in their guides and installers.

Camera binaries and emoji fonts are fetched separately from pinned sources;
checksums identify the inputs and do not grant redistribution rights. Review
upstream licenses before redistributing third-party assets or relicensing files.
